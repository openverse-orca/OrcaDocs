# Welcome to ORCA Lab!

Welcome to the official documentation for the ORCA Lab suite of tools. This guide will walk you through the entire process — from software installation and user registration to using all features, covering quick-start simulation examples, user guides, developer guides, and other core content.

## Product Positioning
ORCA Lab is a high-fidelity physics AI simulation system lightweight edition launched by Songying Technology, focusing on the core philosophy of **"Goodbye complex code — robot training techniques anyone can learn"**. It is designed specifically for university faculty and students, individual users, and non-commercial scenarios. The goal is to lower the barrier to entry for robot virtual simulation training through a streamlined feature layout, while also serving as a promotional gateway for the OrcaStudio Enterprise Edition, allowing users to quickly experience the full core capabilities of the robot training workflow.

---
## Core Advantages
- **Ease of Use**: No complex coding background required. Provides modular, visual operation workflows suitable for university teaching, personal learning, and other non-professional development scenarios, enabling quick onboarding to robot virtual simulation training.
- **Full Workflow Coverage**: From physics AI simulation environment creation and data collection, to data augmentation, cleaning, and model training, then to real-world robot capability transfer — comprehensively covering the key stages of robot virtual simulation training.
- **Flexible Adaptation**: Supports personalized operations such as 3D AI scene generation, custom data processing rules, and diverse model training approaches to meet the experimental and learning needs of different users.

## Target Audience
- **University Faculty & Students**: For robot-related course teaching, lab experiments, academic research, and other scenarios.
- **Individual Users**: For non-commercial robot training technology learning, interest exploration, skill practice, and other scenarios.
- **Prospective Enterprise Users**: For those looking to experience the core capabilities of the ORCA product suite in advance through the lightweight version, providing a reference for future commercial subscription decisions.
