# How to upload custom assets?

## Question
I have created 3D models using other modeling tools, or generated models through OrcaLab's AI generation feature. Now I want to upload these custom assets to the OrcaLab Asset Library for use in the client or to share with others. How should I do this?

## Answer

The OrcaLab Asset Library allows users to upload self-made 3D assets, greatly increasing the platform's flexibility and content diversity. The upload process is primarily done through the Asset Library webpage.

## 📋 Custom Asset Upload Process

### Step 1: Log into the OrcaLab Asset Library

Open your web browser and visit the OrcaLab Asset Library page:

[https://simassets.orca3d.cn/](https://simassets.orca3d.cn/)

And log in with your OrcaLab account.

### Step 2: Enter "Personal Center" or "Tool Center"

-   After logging in, find and click "Personal Center" or "Tool Center" in the left navigation bar of the Asset Library page. If you created the asset through the AI generation feature, there is usually an "Upload Asset" button directly on the generation result page.



### Step 3: Find the "Upload Asset" Entry

-   **From AI Generation Results Page**: If you generated the asset through Text-to-3D or Image-to-3D, after previewing and being satisfied with the result, there will be a clear "Upload Asset" button at the bottom of the page. Click it.
-   **From Personal Center / Tool Center**: If not through AI generation, you may need to find an "Upload Asset" or "Publish Asset Package" entry in "Personal Center" or "Tool Center." This is typically an independent upload form.

### Step 4: Fill in Asset Information and Upload Files

On the upload interface, you need to provide asset-related information and upload the asset file itself.

#### 1. **Select Asset File**
-   Click "Choose File" or "Upload" and select your local 3D model file. OrcaLab typically supports mainstream 3D model formats such as USDZ. Refer to the actual platform for specific supported formats.

#### 2. **Fill in Asset Metadata**
-   **Asset Name**: Give your asset a clear, descriptive name.
-   **Category**: Select the category the asset belongs to (e.g., Industrial Assets, Living Assets, Robot Assets, etc.).
-   **Tags/Keywords**: Add keywords to help other users search.
-   **Description**: Describe the asset's features, usage, source, and other information in detail.
-   **Preview/Cover Image**: Upload a high-quality preview image to serve as the asset's cover.
-   **Version Information**: If version management is available, you can fill in the version number and changelog.

#### 3. **Confirm Copyright and Usage Agreement**
-   Before uploading, you need to read and agree to the asset upload-related agreements, including copyright statements, usage restrictions, etc. Ensure you have the legal rights to the uploaded asset.

### Step 5: Submit for Review (If Required)

-   Some asset uploads may require platform review. After submission, please wait patiently for the review result. Once approved, your asset will be visible in the Asset Library or appear in your personal asset list.

### Step 6: Use in the OrcaLab Client

-   Successfully uploaded assets will appear in your Personal Center. You can subscribe to your own uploaded asset packages in the Personal Center.
-   After subscribing, as with subscribing to any other asset, **close and restart the OrcaLab client**, and the asset will sync locally and appear in the client's "Assets Panel" for your use.



## 💡 Upload Notes

### 1. **Supported File Formats**
- Please consult the OrcaLab official documentation or the Asset Library's upload instructions to confirm supported 3D model file formats. USDZ is a commonly used and recommended format.

### 2. **Asset Quality**
- Uploaded assets should have good model quality, including reasonable polygon counts, clear textures, correct normals and UV mapping, etc.
- For complex robot models, ensure that rigging, joint information, and other configurations are correct.

### 3. **Copyright & Compliance**
- Ensure that the assets you upload do not infringe on any third-party copyrights.
- Asset content must comply with national laws and regulations and the platform's User Agreement.

### 4. **Special Considerations for AI-Generated Assets**
- For assets created through the AI generation feature, after previewing and being satisfied, you can directly click the "Upload Asset" button, and the system will automatically handle most metadata.

### 5. **Asset Package Management**
- If you want to upload multiple related assets, consider packaging them into an asset package for upload, making management and subscription easier.

## 📝 Summary

Uploading self-made assets to the OrcaLab Asset Library provides users with great flexibility and extensibility. Through the "Upload Asset" feature on the Asset Library webpage, fill in the necessary information and upload the file to integrate your custom models into OrcaLab's simulation ecosystem. Be sure to pay attention to asset quality, copyright, and the platform's compliance requirements.
