# How to link third-party accounts?

## Question
I have already registered an OrcaLab account, and now I want to link my account with third-party accounts such as WeChat or GitHub for more convenient login. How should I do this?

## Answer

OrcaLab allows users to link their existing accounts with third-party accounts such as WeChat or GitHub after registration. Once linked, you can use the third-party account to quickly log into OrcaLab without entering your username and password each time.

## 📋 Third-Party Account Linking Process

### Step 1: Log into the OrcaLab Asset Library

Open your web browser and visit the OrcaLab Asset Library page:

[https://simassets.orca3d.cn/](https://simassets.orca3d.cn/)

And log in with your existing OrcaLab account (phone number / email / username and password).

### Step 2: Enter "Account Management" or "Personal Center"

After logging in, you can typically find your "Account Name" or "Personal Center" entry in the upper-right corner or sidebar of the page. Click to enter.



### Step 3: Select the "Link Third-Party Account" Option

On the "Account Management" page, you will see options related to account security and information, which should include "Link Third-Party Account" or a similar option. Click to enter the linking interface.

#### Interface Illustration
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│            Account Management            │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Personal Information                   │
│  Security Settings                      │
│  My Orders (if applicable)              │
│  Link Third-Party Account -------------> Click here
│      WeChat (Not Linked) [Link]        │
│      GitHub (Not Linked) [Link]        │
│  ...                                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Step 4: Complete Third-Party Platform Authorization

Click the "Link" button next to the third-party account you wish to link (such as "WeChat" or "GitHub"). The system will guide you to the corresponding third-party platform for authorization.

#### 🚀 Link WeChat
1.  **Scan QR Code for Authorization**: A QR code will be displayed on the page.
2.  **Operate on Phone**: Use WeChat on your phone to scan the QR code.
3.  **Confirm Authorization**: On your phone's WeChat, confirm authorization for OrcaLab to access your WeChat information.
4.  **Return to OrcaLab**: After successful authorization, the page will automatically redirect back to OrcaLab with a successful linking message.

#### 💻 Link GitHub
1.  **Redirect to Authorization Page**: The page will redirect to GitHub's authorization page.
2.  **Log into GitHub**: If not already logged in, log into your GitHub account first.
3.  **Confirm Authorization**: Review the permissions requested by OrcaLab and click "Authorize" or "Grant" to confirm.
4.  **Return to OrcaLab**: After successful authorization, the page will automatically redirect back to OrcaLab with a successful linking message.

### Step 5: Verify Linking Status

After successful linking, on the "Account Management" page, you will see the corresponding third-party account displayed as "Linked."

## 💡 Advantages of Linking Third-Party Accounts

-   **Convenient Login**: Next time you log in, you can directly click the WeChat or GitHub icon for quick login without entering your username and password.
-   **Account Security**: Third-party platforms typically offer stronger security protections (such as two-factor authentication), indirectly improving your OrcaLab account security.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### 1. **One OrcaLab Account Can Only Link One WeChat/GitHub Account**
- Ensure the third-party account you link is the unique account you wish to associate.

### 2. **Unlinking**
- If you need to unlink, you can find the corresponding "Unlink" option on the "Account Management" page.

### 3. **Third-Party Platform Account Security**
- Ensure your WeChat and GitHub accounts themselves are secure, enabling security measures such as two-factor authentication.

### 4. **Email Binding**
- Even when logging in through a third-party account, the OrcaLab account typically has a bound email (prompted during registration or linking) for receiving notifications and password recovery. Ensure this email is valid and secure.

Through the steps above, you can easily link your OrcaLab account with commonly used third-party platforms and enjoy a more convenient login experience.
