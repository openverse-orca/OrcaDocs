# Can OrcaLab be used for commercial purposes?

## Question
OrcaLab is free simulation software, but can it be used for commercial projects, commercial product development, or profit-generating activities?

## Answer

**OrcaLab explicitly states that it is currently for non-commercial use only.**

## 🚫 Strict Non-Commercial Restrictions

OrcaLab is designed with the philosophy of lowering the barrier to robot virtual simulation training, specifically for university faculty, students, individual users, and non-commercial scenarios. This means that OrcaLab and all its provided features and assets **must not be used for any activities that directly or indirectly generate economic benefit**.

### ❌ Prohibited Commercial Uses

#### Commercial Product Development
- **Prohibited**: Integrating simulation environments, robot models, or training strategies developed using OrcaLab into your commercial products.
- **Prohibited**: Using models or data trained with OrcaLab for the development of commercial software or services.

#### Profit-Generating Projects
- **Prohibited**: Using OrcaLab in any project intended to generate revenue, including but not limited to:
  - Providing paid robot simulation consulting services.
  - Using OrcaLab as a commercial demonstration tool to facilitate sales.
  - Selling or leasing assets or data generated using OrcaLab.

#### Enterprise Production Environments
- **Prohibited**: Deploying and running OrcaLab for daily work in enterprise production lines, R&D departments, testing departments, or other commercial environments.

#### Commercial Training
- **Prohibited**: Using the OrcaLab platform or its content for paid training courses or workshops.

#### Secondary Distribution & Licensing
- **Prohibited**: Any form of commercial distribution, resale, rental, or sublicensing of the OrcaLab software itself, its built-in assets, or assets obtained through the Asset Library.

### ⚖️ Consequences of Violation
- **Account Ban**: If a violation of the non-commercial restriction is discovered, your OrcaLab account may be permanently banned, and you will be unable to continue using related services.
- **Legal Liability**: For serious infringements, Songying Technology reserves the right to pursue legal liability.
- **Data Loss**: All your personal data, created scenes, and subscribed assets may be cleared.

## ✅ Permitted Non-Commercial Scenarios

Despite strict commercial restrictions, OrcaLab is well-suited for the following non-commercial scenarios:

### 🎓 Education & Research
- **University Teaching**: Conduct experiments and demonstrations in robotics, artificial intelligence, and other courses.
- **Academic Research**: Used for non-profit academic projects, thesis validation, and scientific exploration.
- **Student Assignments**: Coursework and capstone projects for undergraduate and graduate students.

### 📚 Personal Learning & Skill Development
- **Interest Exploration**: Personally learn robot simulation technology and cultivate interest.
- **Skill Practice**: Improve personal skills in AI and robotics through practice.
- **Portfolio Building**: Create non-commercial technical portfolios for job seeking or demonstrating personal abilities.

### 🔍 Technical Evaluation
- **Product Pre-Experience**: Enterprise users can conduct technical evaluation and functional testing through OrcaLab before deciding whether to purchase the OrcaStudio Enterprise Edition.

## 💡 Usage Recommendations

- **Clarify Purpose**: Before using OrcaLab, be sure to clarify the nature of your project to ensure it meets non-commercial requirements.
- **Comply with Agreements**: Carefully read and comply with OrcaLab's User Agreement and Privacy Policy.
- **Consult if Unsure**: If you have questions about whether a specific use falls within the commercial category, it is recommended to contact the OrcaLab technical support team in advance for confirmation.

OrcaLab is committed to providing a powerful free tool for learners and researchers. We hope users will understand and comply with its non-commercial restrictions to jointly maintain a positive usage environment.
