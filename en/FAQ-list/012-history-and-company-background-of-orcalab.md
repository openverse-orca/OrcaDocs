# What is the history and company background of OrcaLab?

## Question
Which company developed and launched OrcaLab? What is its development background and vision?

## Answer

OrcaLab was developed and launched by **SongYing Technology**. It is an important component of SongYing Technology's "Orca" product line.

## 🏢 SongYing Technology Company Background

### 1. **Company Name**
- **Chinese Name**: 松应科技
- **English Name**: SongYing Technology

### 2. **Core Mission & Vision**
- SongYing Technology is committed to providing advanced robot AI simulation solutions, empowering robot R&D and application.
- The vision is "Goodbye complex code — robot training techniques anyone can learn," lowering the barrier to robot AI development through technological innovation.
- Promoting the adoption and application of robot technology in education, research, industry, and other fields.

### 3. **Technology Focus Areas**
- **Robot Simulation Technology**: High-fidelity physics simulation engine R&D.
- **Artificial Intelligence (AI)**: AI algorithms for robot perception, decision-making, and control.
- **Virtual Reality (VR)**: Integration of VR/AR with robot interaction.
- **Digital Twin**: Building industrial-grade digital twin solutions.
- **Cloud Services**: Providing an online Asset Library and cloud computing platform.

## 🚀 OrcaLab's Development Background

### 1. **Market Demand Driven**
- With the rapid development of robotics and AI, the demand for efficient, easy-to-use robot simulation training platforms is growing.
- Traditional simulation software has a steep learning curve and high cost, making it unsuitable for beginners and non-professionals.
- Universities and individual users urgently need a low-barrier, fully-featured learning and experimentation tool.

### 2. **An Extension of OrcaStudio Enterprise Edition**
- OrcaLab was created by technologically streamlining and optimizing SongYing Technology's more powerful and comprehensive **OrcaStudio Enterprise Edition**.
- It serves as the "promotional gateway" for the OrcaStudio Enterprise Edition, allowing more users to quickly experience the core capabilities of the Orca product suite.

### 3. **Education Popularization & Talent Development**
- SongYing Technology recognizes the importance of talent development in the robot AI field. By launching OrcaLab, they hope to lower the technical barrier and inspire more people to learn and explore robot AI.

## ✨ OrcaLab's Positioning in the Product Portfolio

### 1. **Entry-Level Product**
- OrcaLab is the **entry-level and popularization product** of the Orca product line.
- Primarily aimed at non-commercial users, providing free access to core features.

### 2. **Technology Validation & Teaching Platform**
- As a technology validation platform, it helps prospective enterprise users evaluate the capabilities of the Orca product suite.
- As a teaching and research tool, it supports university faculty and students in robot-related learning and research.

### 3. **Ecosystem Building**
- Through OrcaLab, attract more developers and users to join the Orca ecosystem, contributing and growing together.

## 🌐 SongYing Technology's Future Outlook

### 1. **Continuous Technological Innovation**
- Continued R&D investment in physics simulation, AI algorithms, VR interaction, cloud computing, and more.
- Continuously improving the performance and user experience of the Orca product suite.

### 2. **Expanding Application Domains**
- Applying robot AI simulation technology to a broader range of fields including industry, services, and education.
- Driving the commercial deployment and industrial upgrading of robot technology.

### 3. **Building an Open Ecosystem**
- Partnering with universities, research institutions, and corporate partners to jointly build a thriving robot AI ecosystem.
- Nurturing the next generation of robot AI talent through products like OrcaLab.

With its deep technological accumulation and forward-looking strategic vision, SongYing Technology is committed to becoming a leader in the robot AI simulation field, and OrcaLab is an important step in this grand vision.
