# What to do if you forget your password?

## Question
If I forget the login password for my OrcaLab account, how can I recover or reset my password?

## Answer

If you forget the login password for your OrcaLab account, don't worry. OrcaLab provides a standard password recovery/reset process so you can easily and quickly set a new password.

## 📋 Password Recovery/Reset Process

### Step 1: Access the Asset Library Page

Open your web browser and visit the OrcaLab Asset Library page:

[https://simassets.orca3d.cn/](https://simassets.orca3d.cn/)

### Step 2: Enter the "Forgot Password" Flow

On the login page, there is usually a "Forgot Password" link or button. Click it.

### Step 3: Verify Your Identity

The system will ask you to provide the **phone number** or **email address** used to register your account to verify your identity.

1.  **Enter Registration Info**: Enter your phone number or email address in the corresponding field.
2.  **Get Verification Code**: Click the "Get Verification Code" or "Send Code" button.
3.  **Receive Verification Code**: Check your phone's SMS inbox or email inbox for the verification code.

#### ⚠️ Notes
-   Ensure the phone number or email address you enter is the one used during registration.
-   If using email recovery, check your **spam/junk folder** in case the verification code email was misclassified.
-   If you haven't received the code after a long time, try resending it or check your network connection.

### Step 4: Enter Verification Code and Set New Password

1.  **Enter Verification Code**: Enter the received verification code into the verification code field on the page.
2.  **Set New Password**: Enter your new password, then enter it again to confirm.
    -   Ensure the new password meets OrcaLab's password strength requirements (typically including uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, with a minimum length requirement).
    -   Ensure the two entries of the new password match exactly.
3.  **Submit**: Click the "Submit" or "Reset Password" button.

### Step 5: New Password Takes Effect — Log In Again

After the password is successfully reset, the system will notify you that your password has been updated. At this point, you can use your **account name / phone number / email** and **new password** to log back into the OrcaLab Asset Library.

## 💡 Password Security Recommendations

### 1. **Set a Strong Password**
- Use a complex password that includes uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Password length of 8 characters or more is recommended.
- Avoid using easily guessed information such as birthdays, names, or phone numbers as passwords.

### 2. **Change Passwords Regularly**
- It is recommended to change your password periodically to improve account security.

### 3. **Do Not Share Passwords**
- Ensure your account password is known only to you and do not share it with others.

### 4. **Use a Password Manager**
- Consider using a professional password manager to generate and store complex passwords for easier management.

### 5. **Link Backup Verification Methods**
- If OrcaLab supports it in the future, consider linking a phone number or secondary email as a backup verification method.

## 📝 Summary

OrcaLab's password recovery process ensures account security through phone or email verification. If you forget your password, simply follow the steps above to easily reset it and log back into your account.
