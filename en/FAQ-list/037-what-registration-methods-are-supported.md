# What registration methods are supported?

## Question
Besides traditional email/phone registration, what convenient third-party registration methods does OrcaLab support for account registration?

## Answer

For user convenience, OrcaLab provides multiple account registration methods, including **phone registration, email registration, and third-party account registration (WeChat and GitHub)**.

## 📋 Registration Methods Supported by OrcaLab

### 1. **Phone Registration**
- **Features**: Register by receiving an SMS verification code on a domestic phone number.
- **Advantages**: Convenient and secure, especially suitable for users in mainland China.
- **Process**:
  1. Select phone registration on the registration page.
  2. Enter your phone number, obtain and enter the SMS verification code.
  3. Fill in personal information (first name, last name, username, password, etc.).
  4. Accept the user agreement and submit the registration.

### 2. **Email Registration**
- **Features**: Register by receiving a verification code via email.
- **Advantages**: Internationally accessible, suitable for users without a domestic phone number or those who prefer email-based account management.
- **Process**:
  1. Select email registration on the registration page.
  2. Enter your email address, obtain and enter the email verification code.
  3. Fill in personal information (first name, last name, username, password, etc.).
  4. Accept the user agreement and submit the registration.

#### ⚠️ Email Registration Notes
- **Gmail Issue**: Due to Google's security policies, Gmail addresses may not receive verification codes. It is recommended to use other email providers (such as QQ Mail, 163 Mail, etc.) as a priority.

### 3. **Third-Party Account Registration**

OrcaLab currently supports quick registration and login through the following two mainstream third-party platforms.

#### 🚀 WeChat Registration
- **Features**: Quick registration and login by scanning a QR code with WeChat for authorization.
- **Advantages**: Very convenient for WeChat users, eliminating the need to fill in tedious information.
- **Process**:
  1. Click the WeChat icon on the registration page.
  2. Use WeChat to scan the QR code displayed on the webpage for authorization.
  3. After successful authorization, the system will guide you to bind an email address (for password recovery and receiving notifications).
  4. Fill in a small amount of necessary personal information (first name, last name, username, phone number, password, etc.).
  5. Accept the user agreement and submit the registration.

#### 💻 GitHub Registration
- **Features**: Register and log in through GitHub account authorization, especially suitable for developers and researchers.
- **Advantages**: Easy to manage, more tightly integrated with development workflows.
- **Process**:
  1. Click the GitHub icon on the registration page.
  2. Enter your GitHub account email and follow the prompts on GitHub's authorization page.
  3. After successful authorization, the system will guide you to bind an email address.
  4. Fill in a small amount of necessary personal information (first name, last name, username, phone number, password, etc.).
  5. Accept the user agreement and submit the registration.

## 📝 Experience After Successful Registration

Regardless of which registration method is used, the system will automatically log you into your OrcaLab account and take you to the Asset Library interface after successful registration. You will gain:
- Access to browse and subscribe to the OrcaLab Asset Library.
- Quota for using the AI 3D asset generation tool.
- Enjoy all non-commercial services provided by OrcaLab.


## 💡 Registration Recommendations

- **Phone/Email Registration**: Recommended if you want fully independent account management or prefer not to link with third-party platforms.
- **WeChat/GitHub Registration**: If you regularly use these platforms, you can register quickly, but you may need to bind a backup email later.
- **Remember Your Password**: Regardless of the method, keep your password safe and choose a strong one.

Choose the registration method that suits you best and start your OrcaLab journey!
