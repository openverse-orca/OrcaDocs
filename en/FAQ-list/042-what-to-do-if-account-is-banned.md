# What to do if your account is banned?

## Question
If my OrcaLab account is banned or disabled by the system for some reason, how should I handle it? What are the possible reasons for a ban?

## Answer

If your OrcaLab account is banned, it typically means you may have violated OrcaLab's User Agreement or Terms of Use. You need to understand the possible reasons for the ban and follow official guidance to appeal or resolve the issue.

## 📋 Common Reasons for Account Bans

OrcaLab clearly defines its usage scope and limitations. Below are common reasons leading to account bans:

### 1. **Violation of Non-Commercial Restrictions (Most Common)**
- **Reason**: Using OrcaLab for commercial purposes, including but not limited to:
  - Use in commercial product development, testing, or integration.
  - Providing paid robot simulation services.
  - Commercial sale or rental of generated assets or data.
  - Deployment and use in enterprise production environments.
- **OrcaLab Is Explicitly Non-Commercial**: OrcaLab is a free lightweight edition launched by Songying Technology, designed exclusively for university faculty/students, personal learning, and non-commercial research. Any commercial use must upgrade to the OrcaStudio Enterprise Edition.

### 2. **Malicious Exploitation or Abuse of AI Generation Quota**
- **Reason**: Using technical means or frequently registering multiple accounts to maliciously exploit the daily free quota for AI 3D asset generation.
- **AI Quota Limits**: OrcaLab has clear daily usage limits for AI generation features.

### 3. **Posting Prohibited Content**
- **Reason**: Posting content on the Asset Library or other OrcaLab-related platforms that contains:
  - Illegal or non-compliant content (such as pornography, violence, politically sensitive material).
  - Content that infringes on others' intellectual property rights.
  - Malware, viruses, or links spreading harmful information.

### 4. **Account Security Issues**
- **Reason**: The account is at risk of being compromised; to protect user security, the system may temporarily ban the account.
- **Indicators**: Unusual login from different locations, account password leaked, frequent failed login attempts, etc.

### 5. **Violation of Other Terms in the User Agreement**
- **Reason**: Any behavior that violates other terms explicitly listed in the OrcaLab User Agreement or Privacy Policy.
- **Examples**: Spreading rumors, malicious attacks, unfair competition, etc.

## 🛠️ Steps to Take After an Account Ban

### Step 1: Stay Calm and Try to Check Notifications

- **Check Email**: First, check the email inbox (including spam/junk folder) associated with your registration. OrcaLab typically sends a notification email explaining the reason for the account ban.
- **Read the Agreement**: Re-read OrcaLab's User Agreement and Terms of Use to understand which rule you may have violated.

### Step 2: Contact OrcaLab Technical Support to Appeal

- **Official Channels**: Reach out to the support team through the technical support channels provided on the OrcaLab official website (such as online customer service, submitting a ticket, official email, etc.).
- **Provide Information**: When appealing, provide as much detail as possible, including:
  - Your OrcaLab registered email or phone number.
  - Your username.
  - The time your account was banned and any notification messages received.
  - The reason you believe your account was banned (if you know).
  - Your explanation of the situation and grounds for appeal.

### Step 3: Cooperate with Investigation and Rectification

- **Actively Cooperate**: The technical support team may request more information or ask you to cooperate with an investigation. Please cooperate actively and explain the situation clearly.
- **Commit to Rectification**: If you did indeed violate the rules, honestly admit it and commit to correcting the mistake and not violating the User Agreement again.
- **If It's a False Ban**: If a system misjudgment led to the ban, provide relevant evidence to explain.

### Step 4: Wait for the Outcome

- **Be Patient**: Account appeals and unbans take some time; please wait patiently for the technical support team's review and response.
- **Stay in Communication**: During this period, continue monitoring your email and maintain communication with technical support.

## 💡 Preventing Account Bans

### 1. **Comply with Terms of Use**
- **Non-Commercial Principle**: Strictly comply with OrcaLab's non-commercial usage regulations, avoiding any commercial use.
- **Fair Use**: Use AI generation quotas reasonably; avoid malicious exploitation.
- **Legal & Compliant**: Do not post or spread any illegal, non-compliant, or infringing content.

### 2. **Protect Account Security**
- **Use Strong Passwords**: Set complex passwords that are hard to guess.
- **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** (if OrcaLab offers it): Further improve account security.
- **Beware of Phishing Links**: Do not click on unknown links; guard against password theft.
- **Regularly Check Your Account**: Monitor for unusual login records.

### 3. **Keep Contact Information Up to Date**
- Ensure your registered email and phone number are current so you can promptly receive system notifications and verification messages.

## 📝 Summary

An account ban is a serious matter, typically caused by violating OrcaLab's User Agreement. Be sure to understand and comply with non-commercial restrictions. If your account is unfortunately banned, immediately contact official technical support to appeal and provide detailed information to cooperate with the investigation.
