# How to register an OrcaLab account?

## Question
As a new user, how should I register an OrcaLab account? What is the registration process? What registration methods are available?

## Answer

Registering an OrcaLab account is the key step to using the OrcaLab Asset Library and enjoying exclusive services. OrcaLab provides two registration methods: **phone/email registration** and **third-party account registration**, with a convenient process.

## 📋 User Registration Guide

### Step 1: Access the Asset Library Page

Open your web browser and visit the OrcaLab Asset Library page:

[https://simassets.orca3d.cn/](https://simassets.orca3d.cn/)

### Step 2: Choose a Registration Method

On the Asset Library login/registration page, you will see registration options. OrcaLab supports two registration methods:

#### Method 1: Phone/Email Registration (Recommended)

This method allows you to create an account using your phone number or email address.

1.  **Click the Register Button**: Usually located below or beside the login interface.
2.  **Enter Phone Number/Email Address**:
    - Enter your commonly used phone number and click to send a verification code.
    - Or enter your commonly used email address and click to send a verification code.
    - **Note**: You will receive a verification code to verify your identity.
3.  **Enter Verification Code**: Check your phone's SMS inbox or email inbox, and enter the received verification code into the designated field.
4.  **Fill in Registration Information**: Follow the prompts to fill in detailed personal information.
    - `First Name`
    - `Last Name`
    - `Username`: Your unique identifier in OrcaLab, displayed in the Personal Center.
    - `Phone Number`: Auto-filled (if registering by phone) or entered manually.
    - `Password`: Set your account password; must be entered twice for confirmation.
    - `Invitation Code` (Optional): If you have an invitation code, you can enter it here.
5.  **Accept Agreements**: Carefully read and check the boxes indicating "I have read and accept the User Agreement, Privacy Policy."
6.  **Submit Registration**: Click the "Submit Registration" button.

#### Method 2: Third-Party Account Registration (WeChat/GitHub)

If you prefer to use an existing social account, OrcaLab supports registration via WeChat or GitHub.

1.  **Click the Third-Party Registration Option**: Select the "WeChat" or "GitHub" icon.
2.  **Complete Authorization as Prompted**:
    - **WeChat**: Use WeChat to scan the QR code displayed on the page for authorization.
    - **GitHub**: Enter your GitHub account email and follow GitHub's authorization flow.
3.  **Proceed to Bind Email**: After successful third-party authorization, the system will guide you to bind an email address.
4.  **Enter Verification Code**: Check your bound email inbox and enter the received verification code into the designated field.
5.  **Fill in Registration Information**: Similar to phone/email registration, fill in:
    - `First Name`
    - `Last Name`
    - `Username`
    - `Phone Number`
    - `Password` (enter twice)
    - `Invitation Code` (Optional)
6.  **Accept Agreements**: Read and check the boxes indicating "I have read and accept the User Agreement, Privacy Policy."
7.  **Submit Registration**: Click the "Submit Registration" button.

### Step 3: Automatic Login After Successful Registration

After successful registration, the system will automatically log into your account and redirect to the Asset Library interface, where you can begin browsing and subscribing to assets.



## ⚠️ Common Registration Issues

### 1. **Email Verification Code Issues**
- **Problem**: If registering with a Gmail address, you may encounter issues receiving the verification code, with a message indicating `The recipient server rejected your email, possibly due to SPF verification failure`.
- **Cause**: Since November 2022, Google requires senders delivering email to Gmail addresses to have SPF or DKIM records configured.
- **Solution**:
  - **Use a non-Gmail email address to register** (such as QQ Mail, 163 Mail, etc.).
  - Or contact your system administrator to configure SPF/DKIM records (this is typically done by technical support personnel).

### 2. **Phone Number/Email Already Registered**
- **Problem**: System indicates your phone number or email is already registered.
- **Solution**:
  - Confirm whether you have previously registered an account. If so, try logging in directly.
  - If you forgot your password, use the password recovery feature.
  - If you are certain you have not registered, contact technical support.

### 3. **Username Already Taken**
- **Problem**: The username you chose is already in use by another user.
- **Solution**: Please choose a different username.

### 4. **Password Does Not Meet Requirements**
- **Problem**: Password strength is insufficient, or the two password entries do not match.
- **Solution**: Set a password that meets the requirements as prompted on the page (typically including uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters, with a minimum length requirement), and ensure both entries match exactly.

## 📝 Summary

OrcaLab's registration process is standard and secure, offering multiple convenient registration methods. If you encounter any issues during registration, please first check the common issue solutions, or contact technical support for assistance.
