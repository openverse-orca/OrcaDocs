# Can one email register multiple accounts?

## Question
How does OrcaLab's registration mechanism work? Can I use the same phone number or email address to register multiple OrcaLab accounts?

## Answer

**OrcaLab generally does not allow a single phone number or email address to register multiple accounts.** This is to ensure the uniqueness of each user, maintain the health and fairness of the account system, and prevent abuse.

## 📋 Registration Mechanism & Uniqueness

### 1. **Phone Number Uniqueness**
- In OrcaLab's phone registration process, one phone number can only be bound to one OrcaLab account.
- If you attempt to register again with an already-registered phone number, the system will prompt that the phone number is already in use.

### 2. **Email Address Uniqueness**
- Similarly, one email address can only be bound to one OrcaLab account.
- Whether registering directly via email or binding an email after registering through a third-party account, this email will serve as the unique identifier and contact method for the account.
- If you attempt to register again with an already-registered email address, the system will prompt that the email is already in use.

### 3. **Username**
- Usernames must also be unique. During registration, if the username you choose is already taken, the system will require you to choose a different one.

## 💡 Why Are Uniqueness Restrictions Implemented?

### 1. **Account Security**
- Ensures each account has clear ownership, facilitating identity verification, password recovery, and security incident tracking.

### 2. **Abuse Prevention**
- Limits the daily free quota for AI 3D asset generation: if a user were allowed to register multiple accounts with the same email, they could easily bypass this limit, causing resource abuse.
- Prevents malicious behavior: reduces the likelihood of spam, fraudulent activities, etc.

### 3. **User Management**
- Maintains clear user profiles and history, facilitating system management and personalized service delivery.

## ⚠️ Common Questions & Solutions

### Q: I have multiple OrcaLab accounts but only one commonly used email. What should I do?
A: If you genuinely need to manage multiple accounts for special reasons (e.g., one for personal learning and one for teaching demonstrations) and wish to use the same email for management, this is generally not permitted. You need to prepare a separate phone number or email address for each account.

### Q: I forgot my account information, but I remember registering with this email. Now I can't register again?
A: This means your email is already bound to an account. You should try using the "Forgot Password" feature to recover your account rather than attempting to register again.

### Q: I want to register a new account with a new email, but it says my previous email is already registered.
A: The OrcaLab system records registration information. If you previously registered with a certain email but now want to use a different new email, make sure the new email hasn't been registered. If you want to change the email on your old account, log into the old account and modify the bound email through the "Account Management" page.

## 📝 Summary

OrcaLab requires the uniqueness of phone numbers and email addresses for each account. This means you cannot use the same phone number or email to register multiple accounts. This policy is designed to safeguard account security and ensure fair use of resources.
