# How to modify personal information?

## Question
I have registered and logged into my OrcaLab account, and now I find that my personal information (such as nickname, phone number, email, etc.) is incorrect or needs updating. How can I modify this personal information?

## Answer

OrcaLab allows users to modify their account's personal information after logging in. These operations are typically performed on the "Account Management" or "Personal Center" page.

## 📋 Personal Information Modification Process

### Step 1: Log into the OrcaLab Asset Library

Open your web browser and visit the OrcaLab Asset Library page:

[https://simassets.orca3d.cn/](https://simassets.orca3d.cn/)

And log in with your account (phone number / email / username and password).

### Step 2: Enter "Account Management" or "Personal Center"

After logging in, click your "Account Name" or the "Personal Center" entry in the upper-right corner or sidebar of the Asset Library page to enter the account management interface.


### Step 3: Select the Information Category to Modify

On the "Account Management" page, you will see different options, typically including:
-   **Personal Information**: Used to modify basic details such as nickname, first name, last name.
-   **Security Settings**: Used to change password, bind phone number / email, etc.

Click the corresponding option based on the specific information you want to modify.

### Step 4: Make Information Modifications

#### 1. **Modify Basic Details (e.g., First Name, Last Name, Username)**
-   Click "Personal Information."
-   Find the field you need to modify (e.g., First Name, Last Name, Username).
-   Enter the new information.
-   Click the "Save" or "Submit" button.
-   **Note**: Usernames typically have uniqueness requirements; modification may require re-verification.

#### 2. **Modify or Bind Phone Number**
-   Click the option related to phone number under "Security Settings" or "Personal Information."
-   You may need to verify your current phone number first (receive a verification code).
-   Enter the new phone number, obtain and enter the new verification code for validation.
-   Click "Save" or "Submit."

#### 3. **Modify or Bind Email**
-   Click the option related to email under "Security Settings" or "Personal Information."
-   You may need to verify your current email first (receive a verification code).
-   Enter the new email address, obtain and enter the new verification code for validation.
-   Click "Save" or "Submit."

#### 4. **Change Password**
-   Click the "Security Settings" option.
-   You typically need to enter your **current password**, then enter the **new password** and confirm the new password.
-   Ensure the new password meets OrcaLab's password strength requirements.
-   Click "Save" or "Submit."

### Step 5: Confirm Modification Results

-   After successful modification, the system typically displays a "Modification successful" message.
-   You can refresh the page or re-enter the "Account Management" page to view your updated personal information.

## 💡 Important Notes

### 1. **Identity Verification**
-   For account security, when modifying sensitive information (such as phone number, email, password), the system typically requires **identity verification**, such as sending a verification code to your currently bound phone or email.

### 2. **Username Uniqueness**
-   The username is typically the account's unique identifier; when modifying, ensure the new username is not already taken.

### 3. **Contact Information Accuracy**
-   Ensure your phone number and email are up-to-date and usable contact methods, which is crucial for receiving verification codes, system notifications, and password recovery.

### 4. **Password Strength**
-   When changing your password, be sure to set a strong password to improve account security.

### 5. **System Synchronization**
-   Certain information modifications (such as phone number or email) may require some time for system synchronization; please be patient.

## 📝 Summary

OrcaLab provides convenient personal information modification functionality, which users can access through the "Account Management" or "Personal Center" in the Asset Library. When modifying sensitive information, be sure to cooperate with identity verification and ensure the accuracy and security of the information.
