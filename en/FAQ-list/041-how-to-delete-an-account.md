# How to delete an account?

## Question
If I decide to stop using OrcaLab, how can I delete my account? What happens to my data after the account is deleted?

## Answer

Deleting an OrcaLab account is an operation that requires careful consideration, as once the account is deleted, all your related data and rights may be permanently removed. OrcaLab typically provides an account deletion process that you can access through the "Account Management" page in the Asset Library.

## 📋 Account Deletion Process

### Step 1: Log into the OrcaLab Asset Library

Open your web browser and visit the OrcaLab Asset Library page:

[https://simassets.orca3d.cn/](https://simassets.orca3d.cn/)

And log in with your account (phone number / email / username and password).

### Step 2: Enter "Account Management" or "Personal Center"

After logging in, click your "Account Name" or the "Personal Center" entry in the upper-right corner or sidebar of the Asset Library page to enter the account management interface.


### Step 3: Find the "Delete Account" Option

On the "Account Management" page, find the "Delete Account" or similar option. This option is usually somewhat hidden and may require careful searching, as it is an irreversible operation.

### Step 4: Read the Deletion Notice and Confirm

Before executing the deletion, the system typically displays a "Deletion Notice" or "Important Notice" dialog detailing the consequences of deleting the account, such as:
-   **Personal Information Removal**: All your personal information will be deleted.
-   **Asset Library Data Loss**: Your subscribed asset list, AI generation records, uploaded custom assets, etc., will be permanently lost.
-   **Cannot Be Recovered**: Once deleted, the account cannot be recovered.
-   **Service Termination**: You will no longer be able to use any services provided by OrcaLab.

You need to carefully read and confirm that you understand all consequences. You may also need to:
-   **Identity Verification**: To ensure it is you performing the operation, the system may require you to re-verify via SMS or email.
-   **Enter Password**: You may be required to re-enter your account password for confirmation.

### Step 5: Complete Deletion

After confirming and completing identity verification, click the "Confirm Deletion" or "Delete Account" button, and your OrcaLab account will be successfully deleted.

## 💡 Consequences of Deleting Your Account

### 1. **Personal Data Removal**
- Your registration information (phone number, email, username, first name, last name, etc.) will be deleted from OrcaLab's servers.

### 2. **Asset Library Data Loss**
- Your subscription records in the Asset Library will disappear.
- Any custom assets you uploaded to the Asset Library (if any) will also be deleted.
- AI 3D asset generation records and works will no longer be visible.

### 3. **Loss of Service Access**
- You will no longer be able to log into the OrcaLab Asset Library.
- You will no longer be able to use the OrcaLab client's online features (such as asset synchronization).

### 4. **Cannot Be Recovered**
- Once an account is deleted, it is typically permanent and cannot be undone. If you wish to use OrcaLab again, you will need to register a new account.

## ⚠️ Recommendations Before Deletion

### 1. **Back Up Important Data**
- Before deleting your account, be sure to back up all locally stored OrcaLab-related project files, custom scripts, layout files, and any simulation data you consider important.

### 2. **Unlink Third-Party Accounts**
- If your OrcaLab account is linked to third-party accounts (such as WeChat, GitHub), it is recommended to unlink them first to avoid potential issues.

### 3. **Clear Local Assets**
- You can clear locally stored asset data by uninstalling the OrcaLab client and manually deleting the local asset directory.

## 📝 Summary

Deleting an OrcaLab account is an irreversible operation that results in the permanent loss of personal information, Asset Library data, and all service access. Before performing the deletion, be sure to back up important data and carefully read the deletion notice to ensure you fully understand the implications.
